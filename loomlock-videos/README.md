# Loomlock videos

Three 60-second videos, 60 fps, H.264 with AAC sound. They share one style: a flat Loomlock blue background, big filled words and schematic motion graphics.

| File | Format | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `loomlock-event-tap-9x16.mp4` | 1080×1920 (9:16) | How Loomlock Experiences works at an event. Guests tap in with a key or with the Locky totem. |
| `loomlock-event-tap-16x9.mp4` | 1920×1080 (16:9) | The same event video with its own horizontal layout: words on the left, visuals on the right. |
| `loomlock-what-is-loomlock.mp4` | 1080×1920 (9:16) | What loomlock is: the app, the keys, the three app locks, the loomlock box, the totem at events and building the habit. |

The app screens are real Loomlock iOS screens. Event and brand names show as "Your event / Your brand", and third-party app icons are blank tiles.
