Game day food is a food group ⚽ Ninety minutes, zero rules.

Rivals on the pitch, friends at the table. Some of the best memories around food don't happen at a perfectly balanced dinner. They happen in the stands with a tray of loaded nachos, at the bar sharing wings with a fan of the other team, or on the stadium steps with empanadas before kickoff.

Match day is about the people, the nerves and the snacks you only eat when your team is playing. You don't need to "make up" for it the next day. One game doesn't undo your habits, and skipping the fun to stay on track isn't the healthy choice it seems.

Win or lose, we eat.
