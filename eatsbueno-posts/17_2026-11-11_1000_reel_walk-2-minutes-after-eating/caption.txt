Walking after eating: just 2 minutes helps your blood sugar 🚶‍♀️

A review of 7 studies found that a light walk of just 2–5 minutes after a meal already helps your body handle the sugar from that meal (Buffey et al., Sports Medicine, 2022).

You don't need a workout. Just walk.
→ After lunch, take one lap around the office
→ After dinner, one lap around the block
→ Make it a call or a chat with someone
