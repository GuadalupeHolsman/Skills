How many steps a day do you really need? 10,000 steps was an ad 👟

In 1965, a Japanese pedometer was sold under the name Manpo-kei, which means "10,000-step meter". It was a brand name, not a scientific rule.

Recent research shows the benefits start with fewer steps than you think and level off as you go up (Paluch et al., Lancet Public Health, 2022).

So today, skip the stress and just add 1,000 more steps than usual:
→ Take your calls on foot
→ Walk to grab lunch
→ One extra lap after dinner
