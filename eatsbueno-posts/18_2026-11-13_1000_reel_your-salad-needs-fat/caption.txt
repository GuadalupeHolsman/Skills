Healthy salad tip: your salad needs fat 🥑

Some vitamins and antioxidants in vegetables, like the ones in tomatoes and carrots, need a little fat to be absorbed.

In one study, adding avocado to a salad greatly increased how much of those antioxidants people absorbed (Unlu et al., Journal of Nutrition, 2005).

So today, add one of these to your salad:
→ Avocado
→ Olive oil
→ Seeds

💾 Save this for your next salad
