Work-life balance includes lunch 📅 Cheesecake between emails, soup before it gets cold.

Busy workdays have a way of pushing food to the bottom of the to-do list. Back-to-back calls, a full inbox, and suddenly it's 3 pm and you've had nothing but coffee. Eating at your desk isn't a failure, but forgetting to eat at all catches up with your energy, focus and mood.

Three small habits that help on busy days:
→ Put lunch on your calendar like any other meeting
→ Keep easy options at work: soup, yogurt, fruit, a sandwich
→ Pause for 10 minutes. Put the call on hold. Your soup is getting cold

And yes, a slice of cheesecake between emails counts as self-care sometimes.

Lunch is on the agenda.
