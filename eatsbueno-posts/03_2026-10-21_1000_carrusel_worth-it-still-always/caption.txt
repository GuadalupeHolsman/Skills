Balanced eating includes the burger. Worth it, still worth it, always worth it 🍔

Somewhere along the way, "healthy" started to mean never ordering the thing you actually want. But a healthy lifestyle isn't built in a single meal. It's built across a week of real food, movement, sleep and time with people you love.

A stuffed sandwich from the corner shop. A cheeseburger at any age. A crispy chicken sandwich on a Friday night. These aren't setbacks. They're part of a life you actually enjoy living.

What balanced eating looks like in practice:
→ Most meals built around protein, fiber and plants
→ Comfort food without the guilt spiral afterwards
→ No "starting over on Monday"

When food stops being a test you pass or fail, it gets a lot easier to make good choices most of the time.
