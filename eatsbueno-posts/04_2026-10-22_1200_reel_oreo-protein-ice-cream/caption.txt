High protein ice cream with Oreos, made in the Ninja Creami 🍫

If you've been looking for a healthy dessert that actually tastes like dessert, this is it. A frozen chocolate protein base, spun in the @ninjakitchen Creami and loaded with @oreo Thins.

How we make it:
1. Take your frozen chocolate protein base out of the freezer
2. Spin it in the Ninja Creami
3. Make a well in the center and add Oreo Thins
4. Run the mix-in function
5. Top with more Oreos and dig in

Creamy, high in protein and still a real treat. Protein desserts work best when they don't feel like a compromise, and this one doesn't.
