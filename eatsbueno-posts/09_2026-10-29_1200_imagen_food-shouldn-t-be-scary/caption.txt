Food anxiety is scarier than any Halloween costume 🎃 Food shouldn't be scary.

If a hot dog at a Halloween party makes your heart race, the problem isn't the hot dog. It's years of hearing that some foods are "bad," "toxic" or "off limits." Those labels turn normal foods into fear foods, and fear around food takes up a lot of space in your head.

A few ways to make food less scary this Halloween weekend:
→ Eat a regular meal before the party, so you don't arrive starving
→ Have the candy or the hot dog you actually want, slowly and without hiding it
→ Notice how you feel afterwards instead of judging yourself

One food never made or broke anyone's health. Context does: what you eat most days, how you sleep, how you move.

If fear of food is taking over your life, a registered dietitian or a therapist can help.

Share this with someone who needs it before Halloween weekend 👻
