Food tracking app for real life: snap any meal, anywhere 📸

Most calorie counters were built for chicken, rice and broccoli. Real life looks different. Homemade waffles on a Sunday. A Colombian soup with your family. Sushi on a Friday night. EatsBueno is a meal tracker made for the way you actually eat.

How it works:
→ Snap your meal
→ EatsBueno breaks it down: not just what's on your plate, but why it matters for your energy and your habits
→ It adapts to your world, your culture and your pace, not the other way around

No food rules, no judgment. Just healthy habits built one real meal at a time, at home or eating out.

If you've ever quit a meal tracker because your food "didn't fit," this one's for you.
