Treat meals are part of a balanced diet: burger, fries and a martini 🍸

The grown-up combo. A juicy burger, a cup of crispy fries, a little dipping sauce and a cold martini, served on a silver tray because you deserve a bit of ceremony.

A treat meal isn't a "cheat." That word implies you're breaking rules, and food isn't a rule book. Treats are part of a normal week, especially when the rest of it is built around real food, movement and rest.

How to enjoy a treat meal without the guilt:
→ Plan it, look forward to it, enjoy every bite
→ Eat slowly, with good company if you can
→ Skip the "starting over on Monday" mindset. There's nothing to start over

Balance isn't the absence of treats. It's knowing they belong.
