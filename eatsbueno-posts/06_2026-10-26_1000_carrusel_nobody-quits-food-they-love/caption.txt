Healthy eating without restriction: nobody quits the food they love, so add a little more 🧀

Every diet that starts with a list of foods you can never eat again ends the same way. You last a few weeks, then you're back where you started, feeling worse about it.

There's a better approach: add instead of subtract.
→ Keep the fried chicken, add a side of veggies
→ Keep the dessert, add protein earlier in the day
→ Keep your favorite meals, add more water, fiber and sleep around them

When you focus on what to add, your plate naturally gets more balanced and the foods you love stop feeling forbidden. That's when cravings calm down and habits actually stick.

It's all bueno.
