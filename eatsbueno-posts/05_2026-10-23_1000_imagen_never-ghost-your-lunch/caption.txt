Healthy lunch ideas for work start with one rule: never ghost your lunch 👻

Skipping lunch feels productive until 4 pm hits. Low energy, zero focus, and suddenly you're raiding the snack drawer or ordering whatever arrives fastest. Skipping meals doesn't make you more disciplined. It just moves the hunger to later, when it's harder to make good choices.

A simple formula for a workday lunch that keeps you going:
→ A protein: chicken, eggs, beans, fish or tofu
→ Something with fiber: veggies, salad, whole grains or fruit
→ Something you genuinely enjoy, even if it's a burger on a busy day

It doesn't need to be perfect or homemade. It just needs to happen.

Block 20 minutes in your calendar today and treat it like a meeting you can't miss.
