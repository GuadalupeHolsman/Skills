What I eat in a day on a road trip to Area 51 👽

Eating healthy while traveling doesn't mean packing sad salads. It means planning a few anchors and enjoying the rest. Here's everything I ate driving the Extraterrestrial Highway:

☕ Road snack: a café latte protein shake
💧 Electrolytes, because it's the desert
🥪 Lunch at the Little A'Le'Inn: club sandwich with coleslaw and alien hot sauce
🍫 A protein bar in the car to hold me over
🥙 Dinner: a chicken pita and hot tea
🍳 Next morning: eggs, sausage and coffee
🍗 Panda Express for the road

Protein at every stop, enough water, and zero stress about the diner food. Road trip food can be balanced without being boring.

Road trips can still be bueno.
