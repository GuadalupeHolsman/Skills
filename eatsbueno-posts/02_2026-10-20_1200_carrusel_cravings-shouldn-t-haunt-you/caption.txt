How to stop food cravings from running your day 👻 Swipe for the 3 food rules we're letting die this Halloween.

Cravings get louder when you restrict. Skip meals, ban your favorite foods, "earn" dinner at the gym, and your brain spends the whole day thinking about food. That isn't a lack of willpower. It's what happens when your body isn't getting enough.

3 food rules to leave in the haunted house:
1. "Cravings mean I'm weak." Cravings are information. Regular, satisfying meals make them quieter.
2. "I have to earn my dinner." Food isn't a reward for exercise. You eat because you're alive.
3. "Training days mean eating less." Lift, eat, repeat. Muscles need fuel to recover.

Intuitive eating starts with dropping the rules that keep you stuck between restricting and overeating.
