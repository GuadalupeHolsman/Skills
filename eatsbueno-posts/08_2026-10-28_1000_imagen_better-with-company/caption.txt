Eating with friends is part of a healthy relationship with food 🍔

We spend a lot of time talking about what's on the plate and almost none about who's around the table. But sharing burgers and fries with people you love does something no meal plan can: it makes food feel like connection instead of a test.

Social eating isn't the enemy of your goals. Skipping dinners out, saying no to every invitation and eating alone to "stay on track" usually backfires. Balance is being able to enjoy the night and get back to your routine the next day, without compensating.

A few ideas for eating out with friends:
→ Order what you actually want
→ Eat slowly and enjoy the conversation
→ Stop when you're satisfied, not when the plate is empty

Food is always better with company.
