Comfort food without the apology: fries and bacon are bueno too 🍟

Crispy fries. Sizzling bacon. Two foods that have been on every "never eat" list for decades. And yet, they show up at the best breakfasts, the best burger nights and the best memories.

Here's the thing about balanced eating: no single food decides your health. Your overall pattern does. What you eat most days, how much you move, how well you sleep. Fries on a Friday or bacon on a Sunday morning fit into that picture just fine.

What doesn't help is the apology that comes after. The guilt, the "I'll be good tomorrow," the skipped meal to compensate. That cycle is harder on your habits than the fries ever are.

So enjoy them. Pair them with a protein or some greens if you want. And move on.
